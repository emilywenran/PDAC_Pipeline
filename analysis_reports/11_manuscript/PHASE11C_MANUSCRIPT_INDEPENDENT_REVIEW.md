# Phase 11C: Independent Manuscript Claim-Control Review

## Executive Summary
This document summarizes the independent claim-control review of the Phase 11B manuscript draft. The review verifies that the manuscript adheres strictly to computational evidence constraints and avoids biological overstatement, particularly regarding causality, spatial validation, and prioritization logic.

## Review Domains and Assessment

1. **Microbial causality overstatement**: PASS. The text correctly notes that "No causal relationship between Ochrobactrum and the observed host transcriptional programmes is established or claimed".
2. **Microbial localization overstatement**: PASS. The text explicitly states that "spatial localisation of Ochrobactrum within tumour tissue was not determined".
3. **Physical interaction overstatement**: PASS. The text confirms "No physical interaction between Ochrobactrum and host cells is proposed".
4. **Full spatial-validation overstatement**: PASS. The association of HALLMARK_PROTEIN_SECRETION with the basal-classical axis is correctly classified as `PARTIAL_SPATIAL_SUPPORT`.
5. **CTCFL/BORIS over-promotion**: PASS. CTCFL is explicitly stated as being penalized due to `CELL_COMPOSITION_EXPLAINED` sensitivity and is not prioritized.
6. **Literature rescue of unsupported targets**: PASS. Prioritization is based solely on the data-driven framework.
7. **Hidden null or partial findings**: PASS. Null findings including HALLMARK_SPERMATOGENESIS, MEred, MEpurple, and 9 TF activities are reported.
8. **Missing rCLR sensitivity and contamination limitations**: PASS. The text discloses that 8/9 genera were sensitive to rCLR, and Herbaspirillum is a contaminant.
9. **Missing Moncada exploratory-status limitation**: PASS. Moncada is accurately described as exploratory with 1/6 sections showing positive concordance.
10. **Inconsistent claim-to-evidence mapping**: PASS. Claims in the manuscript accurately trace back to the evidence map.
11. **Unsupported therapeutic or clinical claims**: PASS. Targets are presented as hypotheses for follow-up rather than established therapeutic targets.
12. **Missing limitations in Abstract, Results, or Discussion**: PASS. Limitations are adequately integrated into the text (e.g., in the abstract as "composition-sensitive caveats").

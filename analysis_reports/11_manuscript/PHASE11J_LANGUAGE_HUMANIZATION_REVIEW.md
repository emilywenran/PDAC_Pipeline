# PHASE11J: Language Humanization Review

## Sections Edited
The humanization review focused primarily on the **Abstract**, **Introduction**, **Discussion**, and **Limitations** sections. 
The **Methods Summary**, **Results**, and **Figure/Table Legends** received minimal to no edits to preserve their technical precision and avoid altering the underlying scientific meaning.

## Main Types of Stylistic Changes
1. **Reduced AI-style academic phrasing**: Replaced generic transition words (e.g., "Critically, however," "Notably") and formulaic phrases (e.g., "provides valuable insights into," "These findings highlight") with more direct and concise alternatives.
2. **Simplified repetitive sentence structures**: Combined or restructured sentences in the Abstract and Discussion to improve the natural flow of the text, avoiding consecutive sentences with identical grammatical patterns.
3. **Varied syntax**: Adjusted sentence length to create a more engaging, researcher-written tone while maintaining a formal academic style.
4. **Enhanced directness**: Rephrased certain conclusions to be more definitive without strengthening the underlying scientific claims (e.g., "Our analysis demonstrates" instead of "Our integrative approach highlights").

## Sentences Intentionally Left Unchanged
Several passages in the **Results** section, particularly those detailing specific gene-set enrichments, single-cell decomposition results, and multi-layer scoring penalties, were left unchanged. Rewriting these sentences risked inadvertently altering the precise scientific meaning, altering numerical results, or introducing stylistic inconsistency in highly technical paragraphs.

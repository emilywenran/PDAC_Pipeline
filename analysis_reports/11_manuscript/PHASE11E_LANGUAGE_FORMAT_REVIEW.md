# Phase 11E Language and Format Review Report

## Objective
Conduct a language, structure, and formatting review of the full manuscript draft (`PHASE11D_FULL_MANUSCRIPT_DRAFT.md`), resulting in an edited version (`PHASE11E_FULL_MANUSCRIPT_LANGUAGE_EDITED.md`). The review must strictly adhere to all prior epistemic constraints and claim-control boundaries, including those established in Phases 11C and 11D.

## Method
A line-by-line review was conducted to improve readability, paragraph flow, terminology consistency, and to reduce repetition. The review was strictly constrained:
- **No new biological analyses** were run.
- **No evidence categories** were changed.
- **No target rankings** were altered.
- **No scientific conclusions** were added.
- **No null or partial findings** were removed.
- **No previous Phase 11C or Phase 11D decisions** were overruled.

## Edits Performed

### 1. Abstract
- Tightened the background statement.
- Expanded the methods sentence to explicitly mention bulk, single-cell, spatial transcriptomics, and the external validation layer.
- Split dense results reporting for readability.
- Standardised `CELL_COMPOSITION_EXPLAINED` terminology.

### 2. Introduction
- Consolidated the repeated microbial non-causality disclaimer into a single, comprehensive compound sentence to reduce repetition without weakening the constraint ("it has not been established that Ochrobactrum causes, is spatially localised with, or physically interacts with...").
- Shortened paragraph transitions.
- Replaced project-internal phase references with descriptive text (e.g., "the corrected multi-layer scoring framework").

### 3. Results (Sections 2.1 - 2.6)
- **2.1 Cohort Characteristics:** Transitioned to active voice and reduced redundant disclaimers via forward reference.
- **2.2 Subtype Landscape:** Tightened wording.
- **2.3 Gene-Set Enrichment:** Improved bullet formatting for `HALLMARK_PROTEIN_SECRETION`. Explicitly preserved `PARTIAL_SPATIAL_SUPPORT` and exploratory 1 of 6 section concordance for Moncada. Kept all null findings (`HALLMARK_SPERMATOGENESIS`, `MEred`, `MEpurple`, 9 TFs) explicit.

- **2.5 Regulatory Candidates:** Tightened wording. Preserved exclusion of `CTCFL/BORIS` due to `CELL_COMPOSITION_EXPLAINED` without introducing rescue language.
- **2.6 Target Prioritisation:** Clarified without adding new conclusions. Replaced internal phase labels.

### 4. Discussion, Limitations, Methods Summary, Legends
- Expanded the Discussion into coherent manuscript-style paragraphs while strictly maintaining statistical association boundaries.
- Softened causal-adjacent language ("their causal direction cannot be inferred").
- Maintained the single-cohort primary design limitation (n=62), no-causality, no-localisation, and no-physical-interaction limitations.
- Expanded Methods summary.
- Expanded Figure and Supplementary Table legends to 2-3 sentences. Standardised UK spelling (tumour, localisation, prioritisation, etc.).
- Visibly stated `PARTIAL_SPATIAL_SUPPORT` and Moncada limitations in relevant legends (Figure 5, Table S3).
